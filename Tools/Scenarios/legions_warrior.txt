# L4 step 7 (Galadriel): the Legions' Damned Warrior and its necrotic rage button, through REAL gestures. A Barracks of the Legions, a Damned Warrior, an Aube Spearman close by; you click the warrior on its body (hol.Select.ClickUnit), the grid shows the Rage cell, you click it (hol.HUD.Click GridCell02: the same path as the mouse, then the order goes through the server like any order), the rage runs 10 s, then it ends. Nothing in the drawing marks the rage (no aura, no colour: see Retours\Q-legions-warrior-rage-visual.md), so the effect is read in the log (cast line) and in the warrior's health bar.
