# D788 (Q-income-tooltip-rate = C): over a resource of the top bar, its income a minute, corrected for the trips to the drop-off.
# The hover as the mouse does it: hol.HUD.HoverIncome <0 food | 1 wood | 2 stone | 3 gold> hit-tests the centre of the amount by Slate's own
# window hit test, logs the tooltip the engine would show ("HUD income hover <i>: <text>") and draws that same tooltip widget in the game
# viewport under the amount (the engine shows it in a window of its own, which the capture of the viewport does not see); -1 removes it.
# Exact texts (read in the .po files and fill_po.py, 2026-11-13): FR "+{0} {1}/min" with "bois"; EN "+{0} {1}/min" with "wood"; JA "+{0} {1}/分" with "木材".
# Plain, you (slot 0) against a frozen AI (slot 1). Your Peasants are sent to the wood nearest your town centre.
waitlog Starting hero of slot 0 spawned at
hol.AI.ThinkSteps 100000
hol.UI.Language fr
hol.Gather.LogDeposits 1
hol.Gather.SpeedScale 5
hol.Units.Gather -14221 -1816 0
# The rate is the gatherers' while they gather: wait for the first unloads so the income is published.
waitlog Gather: slot 0
# 30 frames, not 120: on a saturated machine 120 frames outlived the step's timeout (preflight 2026-11-13 18:25).
frames 30
hol.Camera.FocusNear 0 0
hol.Camera.Zoom -2
frames 10
# 1. FR: wood income over the wood amount; no wood income would read "+0 bois/min".
hol.HUD.HoverIncome 1
waitlog HUD income hover 1: +
waitlog  bois/min
frames 5
capture income_tip_fr
# 2. Gold: no gatherer and no sacred site, the tooltip says +0.
hol.HUD.HoverIncome 3
waitlog HUD income hover 3: +0 or/min
frames 5
capture income_tip_gold_fr
# 3. EN, the same hover after a live language switch.
hol.UI.Language en
frames 10
hol.HUD.HoverIncome 1
waitlog  wood/min
frames 5
capture income_tip_en
# 4. JA: "/分", not "/min".
hol.UI.Language ja
frames 10
hol.HUD.HoverIncome 1
waitlog  木材/分
frames 5
capture income_tip_ja
hol.HUD.HoverIncome -1
hol.UI.Language fr
note Survole un montant de la barre du haut (bois, or...) : l'infobulle donne le revenu par minute de cette ressource, trajets compris.
# The scenario's real end: an agent's play.ps1 -Hidden closes here instead of at its -TimeoutSec; the user's own run ignores it and plays on.
quit
